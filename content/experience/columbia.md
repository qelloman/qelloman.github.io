# Columbia University

## Graduate Research Assistant (Sep 2013 — Dec 2018)
**Advisor**: Mingoo Seok

### SRAM-ADC
- Designed a novel SRAM architecture transformable to a stochastic ADC with 6-bit level in 65nm
- Repurposed bitcells as gain stages and linearized ADC output with <1% area overhead
- Published in IEEE SSCL 2019

### Event-driven Digital LDO
- Designed continuous-time ADC and trigger generator for latency reduction
- Implemented and optimized digital PI controller algorithm for voltage regulation
- Achieved 74x FoM improvement and 4x area reduction over 3 prototyped designs in 65nm
- Published at **ISSCC 2016, 2017** and **IEEE JSSC 2017**

### Analytical Model of Energy-Optimal Voltage
- Derived analytical solution of energy-optimal voltage by key technology parameters for VDD scaling
- Verified the models with <10% error across different technology nodes (130nm-45nm)
- Published at IEEE ICCD 2015
