# CRONuS

Circuit Rapid Optimization with Neural Simulator.

## Overview
A neural simulator approach to estimate circuit performance distributions under process variation, enabling robust analog circuit design without numerous Monte Carlo simulations.

## Highlights
- Model-based reinforcement learning for analog circuits (OpAmp, TIA, Comparators)
- Neural simulator replaces costly MC simulations for local variation analysis
- 5x sample efficiency improvement to meet target specifications

## Publication
Published at **IEEE Design, Automation & Test in Europe (DATE) 2024**
