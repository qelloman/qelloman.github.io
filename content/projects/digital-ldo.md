# Event-Driven Digital LDO

Fully Integrated Digital Low-Dropout Regulator based on Event-Driven PI Control.

## Overview
A novel digital LDO architecture using event-driven control for fully integrated voltage regulation, eliminating the need for large external capacitors.

## Highlights
- Event-driven self-triggering control architecture
- 0.5V input, 165-mA/mm2 current density
- 74x FoM improvement over 3 prototyped designs in 65nm
- 4x area reduction with fully integrated 100pF output capacitor
