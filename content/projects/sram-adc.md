# SRAM-ADC

SRAM Circuits Transformable to a Stochastic ADC.

## Overview
A novel SRAM architecture that can dual-function as a 6-bit stochastic ADC with minimal area overhead, enabling in-memory computation capabilities.

## Highlights
- Designed in 65nm CMOS technology
- Repurposed SRAM bitcells as gain stages
- Linearized ADC output by pre-characterization
- Less than 1% area overhead

## Publication
Published in **IEEE Solid-State Circuits Letters (SSCL) 2019**
